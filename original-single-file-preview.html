A few helpful answers

Before we walk you through AIMS.

Every accommodation business runs a little differently. A personalized demo helps you explore the workflows and integrations relevant to your operation.

What kinds of accommodation businesses can use AIMS?

AIMS is being positioned for hotels, lodges, resorts, guesthouses, apartments, and operators managing multiple properties. The right configuration depends on your operational needs.

Can AIMS work with our existing website or app?

AIMS includes a booking-system integration direction for websites and apps. During a demo, we can discuss your current setup and confirm the integration approach available for your project.

Does AIMS support OTAs, channel management, and POS?

These are important parts of the platform direction. Channel coverage, synchronization, and POS availability should be confirmed with the AIMS team based on current implementation status.

Can we see the modules that matter to our business?

Yes. The demo can focus on relevant areas such as property operations, company oversight, finance, inventory, staff access, and investor management.

What is the AIMS hospitality marketplace?

It is a separate marketplace concept intended to connect accommodation businesses with product suppliers and service providers, from equipment and supplies to DJs, live bands, and professional services. Availability and launch details are to be confirmed.

How do we get started?

Book a demo and tell us about your property or group. The team can walk you through the relevant workflows and discuss setup, integrations, and next steps.
